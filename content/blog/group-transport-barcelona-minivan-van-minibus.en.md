# Group Transport in Barcelona: When to Choose a Minivan, XL Van or Minibus with Driver

**The short answer:** for **families and small groups** with normal luggage, a **minivan with driver** is usually enough. Once your group fills a passenger vehicle, or brings bulky luggage, step up to an **XL van**. Like any chauffeured vehicle without a bus licence, it can have **no more than 9 seats including the driver**. Beyond that you need a **minibus**, and in Spain that means the operator must hold a **VD authorisation for passenger transport by bus**. Getting this right isn't only about comfort; it's about staying legal.

Regulatory details and third-party fares below are indicative and were checked **as of October 2026**.

## The 9-seat rule: what to know before you book

Spanish transport law draws a clear line:

- **Vehicles with up to 9 seats, including the driver:** cars, minivans and vans. These can operate as chauffeured hire vehicles (VTC) or taxis.
- **Vehicles with more than 9 seats, including the driver:** legally classed as buses, even if they're minibuses. Carrying paying passengers in them requires a **VD authorisation**, which in Catalonia is issued by the Generalitat (the regional government).

In practice, **no chauffeured van without a VD authorisation can carry more than 8 passengers**. If someone offers to squeeze ten people into a van with driver "no problem", be wary: it doesn't comply with the rules.

When booking a minibus, always ask the operator to confirm it holds a valid VD authorisation.

## Taxi, minivan, XL van or minibus: how to decide

### Taxis: only for very small groups

The Barcelona metropolitan area does have taxis with more than five seats, but you can't always get one when you need it. Under the 2026 AMB fares, a **€4.60 supplement** applies when more than four passengers travel in a vehicle with over five seats. Splitting a group across several taxis means staggered arrivals, multiple payments and the risk of someone going astray.

### Minivan with driver

Ideal for **families, small business teams or couples travelling heavy**. Comfortable, low-key and easy to manoeuvre through the old town's narrow streets. Exact capacity depends on the model, so tell us your headcount and luggage and we'll confirm the right vehicle. More on our [minivan transfer](/en/minivan-transfer-barcelona) page.

### XL van with driver

The go-to for **groups at the upper limit of a passenger vehicle**, or slightly smaller groups with bulky kit: golf bags, buggies, trade show materials or cruise-length luggage. See our [XL van transfer](/en/xl-van-transfer-barcelona) service.

### Minibus with driver

When your group outgrows a van, a minibus keeps everyone together in one vehicle. It's the standard choice for **congresses, incentive trips, weddings and full-day excursions**. Minibuses come in different sizes; ask us about your group on our [minibus hire](/en/minibus-hire-barcelona) page.

## FAQ

### How many passengers can a van with driver carry?
No more than 8, because a chauffeured vehicle without a bus licence cannot exceed 9 seats including the driver. Actual capacity also depends on the model and the luggage.

### What is a VD authorisation?
It's Spain's licence for discretionary public passenger transport by bus, required to carry paying passengers in vehicles with more than 9 seats including the driver.

### Is one XL van better than two minivans?
If everyone fits with their luggage, one XL van keeps the group together and makes coordination easier. If people arrive at different times or head to different places, two vehicles give you more flexibility.
