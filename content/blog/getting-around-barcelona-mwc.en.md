# Getting Around Barcelona During MWC: A Transport Guide for Attendees and Exhibitors

**The short answer:** MWC takes place at **Fira Gran Via** in L'Hospitalet de Llobregat, not in central Barcelona. Your fastest public transport options are **metro line L9 Sud** (stops *Europa | Fira* and *Fira*) straight from the airport, or **FGC trains from Plaça Espanya** (L8, S3, S4, S8, S9, R5, R6, R50 and R60 to *Europa | Fira*). If you're carrying luggage or booth materials, or running a back-to-back meeting schedule, a **pre-booked private transfer** or an **hourly chauffeur** will spare you the taxi queues and crowded platforms that define show week.

According to the GSMA's official site, **MWC Barcelona 2027 runs from 1 to 4 March 2027** at Fira Gran Via. Always double-check dates and opening hours on mwcbarcelona.com before you book flights and hotels.

All third-party figures below (fares, lines, schedules) are indicative and were checked **as of October 2026**.

## Where Fira Gran Via is, and why planning matters

Fira Gran Via sits at Av. Joan Carles I, 64, L'Hospitalet de Llobregat, roughly 12 km from Barcelona–El Prat Airport according to Fira de Barcelona. Unless you're staying right next door, you'll be commuting every day of the show.

The venue has several entrances, and which one you use matters. At MWC 2026, main MWC attendees used the **South entrance (MWC Entrance)**, closest to *Europa | Fira* station, while **4YFN attendees used the North entrance**, nearer to *Fira* (L9 Sud) and *Foc* (L10 Sud). Check which entrance your pass is linked to; turning up at the wrong one can mean a long walk.

The **Talent Arena** has been held at **Fira Montjuïc** next to Plaça Espanya, with an electric shuttle bus linking both venues during show days. If your agenda spans both sites, build that into your timings.

## Public transport: metro and FGC trains

### Metro L9 Sud: the airport-to-venue line

**L9 Sud** connects Terminals 1 and 2 directly with *Fira* and *Europa | Fira*, with no changes. Travel guides put the airport-to-Fira journey at around 22 minutes, with trains roughly every 7 minutes.

Worth knowing before you go:

- The standard **T-casual card is not valid at the L9 Sud airport stations**. You need the **Airport ticket**, priced at **€5.90** in 2026 according to TMB (or a pass that includes airport travel).
- In recent editions, MWC has given attendees a **free transport pass** covering metro, buses and Zone 1 FGC trains, including one airport journey, available at the airport or at the venue. Check the 2027 conditions on the official website.
- Platforms get packed at opening and closing times. With a large suitcase or demo kit, it's hard work.

### From the city centre: L1, FGC L8 and connections

- **From Plaça Espanya:** FGC trains (L8 and the S/R Llobregat–Anoia services) reach *Europa | Fira* in a few stops. Ideal if you're staying in the lower Eixample, Sant Antoni or Poble-sec.
- **On the red line L1:** change to L9 Sud at **Torrassa**, or get off at *Espanya* and switch to FGC. MWC's own guidance also mentions changing to L9 at **Collblanc** (L5).
- **L10 Sud:** *Foc* station is handy for the North entrance.

In 2026, a single metro ticket costs **€2.90** and a **T-casual** (10 journeys, Zone 1) **€13**. If you don't have the MWC pass, the T-casual is usually the better deal over several show days.

## Taxis at MWC: fares and queues

Taxis are fine for the odd journey, but show week tests their limits.

**Official AMB taxi fares for 2026** (valid 19 January 2026 to 17 January 2027):

- Flag fall: **€2.80**.
- Per kilometre: **€1.35** (T-1, weekdays 8am–8pm) and **€1.66** (T-2, nights, Saturdays and public holidays).
- Waiting time: **€27.75 per hour**.
- Airport supplement: **€4.60**, with a **€21 minimum fare** for journeys starting at the airport.
- **€4.60** supplement for more than four passengers in vehicles with over five seats.

**The queues.** At MWC 2026, the official taxi rank was at the South entrance, with operating hours set by the organisers. When the halls close, tens of thousands of people leave at once, so waiting is normal. If you have a client dinner booked for a fixed time, don't leave it to the rank.

## Where to stay and indicative travel times

These are **rough driving estimates in normal traffic**; at show opening and closing times they can stretch considerably:

- **Plaça Europa / Gran Via L'Hospitalet:** minutes from the South entrance. In high demand and among the first areas to sell out.
- **Plaça Espanya and Poble-sec:** around 10–15 minutes by car, or a few FGC stops.
- **Eixample and Passeig de Gràcia:** around 20–30 minutes by car.
- **Diagonal Mar / 22@:** around 25–35 minutes by car; the metro means changing lines.
- **Castelldefels or Sitges:** quieter bases, but you'll depend on the car or train and on traffic on the C-31/C-32.
