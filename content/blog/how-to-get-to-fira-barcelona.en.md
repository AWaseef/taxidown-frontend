# How to Get to Fira Barcelona (Gran Via and Montjuïc) from the Airport and City Centre

**The short answer:** Fira de Barcelona runs two venues. **Fira Gran Via** in L'Hospitalet is linked **directly to the airport by metro line L9 Sud** (stops *Fira* and *Europa | Fira*) and to Plaça Espanya by **FGC trains**. **Fira Montjuïc** sits right by **Plaça Espanya** (metro L1 and L3), which is also an **Aerobús** stop. Travelling with booth materials, samples or demo equipment? A **door-to-door private transfer** in a properly sized vehicle is usually the least painful option.

Third-party prices and timetables below are indicative and were checked **as of October 2026**.

## Two venues: check which one is yours

It sounds obvious, but it's the most common mistake: confirm which venue your trade show or congress is in. They are not within walking distance of each other.

- **Fira Gran Via:** Av. Joan Carles I, 64, 08908 L'Hospitalet de Llobregat. Home to the big international shows, including MWC. Fira de Barcelona puts it about 12 km from the airport.
- **Fira Montjuïc:** Av. Reina Maria Cristina, s/n, 08004 Barcelona, at the foot of Montjuïc hill beside Plaça Espanya. More central, with historic exhibition halls.

If your event uses both sites, the simplest link is **FGC between Plaça Espanya and Europa | Fira**: according to Fira de Barcelona, every FGC line from Espanya stops at Europa | Fira.

## Airport to Fira Gran Via

### Metro L9 Sud: no changes needed

This is the direct link. L9 Sud leaves from Terminals 1 and 2 and stops at *Fira* and *Europa | Fira*. Travel guides estimate around 22 minutes, with trains roughly every 7 minutes.

- **Ticket:** the T-casual isn't valid at the airport stations. You'll need the **Airport ticket (€5.90 in 2026, per TMB)**.
- **Which stop:** it depends on your show's entrance. At MWC 2026, for instance, the main South entrance was next to *Europa | Fira*.

### Bus 46

Fira de Barcelona lists **TMB bus 46**, stopping at Pl. Europa, as the direct bus from the airport. It's cheap, but slower than the metro and awkward with lots of luggage.

### Taxi

It's a short ride, but the meter depends on traffic. Under the **2026 AMB fares**: €2.80 flag fall, €1.35/km on T-1 (weekdays 8am–8pm) or €1.66/km on T-2, a **€4.60 airport supplement** and a **€21 minimum** from the airport. During major shows, expect queues at the airport taxi rank.

## Airport to Fira Montjuïc

- **Aerobús:** lines A1 (T1) and A2 (T2) stop at **Plaça Espanya**, a short walk from the venue. A single costs **€7.45** and a return **€12.85**, according to Aerobús, with daytime departures roughly every 5–10 minutes.
- **Metro:** L9 Sud to Torrassa, then L1 to *Espanya*, using the Airport ticket.
- **Rodalies train (R2 Nord):** the station is next to T2 (a shuttle bus runs from T1). It takes you to Sants, where you switch to metro L3 for Espanya. Fare including the airport supplement: **[VERIFY]**.
- **Taxi or private transfer:** straight to the venue door, no changes.

## From the city centre

### To Fira Gran Via

- **FGC from Plaça Espanya:** lines L8, S3, S4, S8, S9, R5, R6, R50 and R60 to *Europa | Fira*. Fast and frequent.
- **Metro L1:** change to L9 Sud at **Torrassa**.
- **L10 Sud:** *Foc* station is close to the venue's North entrance.
- **Bus:** Fira lists TMB lines H12, V1, 46, 65 and 79.
- **Chauffeured car or taxi:** from the Eixample, allow roughly 20–30 minutes in normal traffic (our estimate).

### To Fira Montjuïc

- **Metro L1 and L3:** *Espanya* station.
- **Bus:** plenty of TMB routes serve the area, including H12, H16, V7, D20, 13, 23, 46, 65 and 79.
- **On foot:** from Sant Antoni, Poble-sec or the lower Eixample, it's an easy walk.

In 2026, a single metro ticket costs **€2.90** and a T-casual (10 journeys, Zone 1) **€13**.

## Practical tips for exhibitors travelling with kit

Arriving with a carry-on is one thing. Arriving with brochure boxes, screens, pop-up banners and demo units is quite another. Here's what tends to make the difference.

### 1. Size the vehicle by volume, not just headcount

Three people with three suitcases fit in a car; three people with booth materials may not. A [minivan with driver](/en/minivan-transfer-barcelona) or an [XL van transfer](/en/xl-van-transfer-barcelona) gives you room for boxes without splitting the team across several taxis. Share rough dimensions when you book.

### 2. Keep hand-carried kit and freight separate

What travels with you (samples, laptops, giveaways) can go in the vehicle. Heavy or palletised freight should go through the logistics channels set out in the organiser's **exhibitor manual**, including loading-bay access and build-up schedules.

### 3. Plan around build-up and breakdown days

During build-up, venue access points and unloading areas may be restricted. Check the exhibitor manual for which gate to use and whether your vehicle needs a pass.

### 4. Agree an exact pick-up point

At a venue the size of Gran Via, "at the entrance" isn't enough. Name the specific entrance (South or North, for example) and confirm the meeting point with your driver.

### 5. Leave slack on the way home

On the last day, breakdown, taxi queues and airport runs all overlap. An [hourly chauffeur](/en/hourly-chauffeur-barcelona) lets you load your kit, stop by the hotel and still reach the airport calmly.

## FAQ

### What's the fastest way from the airport to Fira Gran Via?
By public transport, metro L9 Sud runs direct to *Fira* or *Europa | Fira*. Door to door, a taxi or private transfer, especially if you have luggage.

### How do I get between Fira Gran Via and Fira Montjuïc?
Take FGC between *Europa | Fira* and *Plaça Espanya*, a short walk from Fira Montjuïc. Some events, such as MWC, have also run shuttle buses between the two venues.

### Can I use a T-casual from the airport?
Not at the L9 Sud airport stations. You'll need the Airport ticket (€5.90 in 2026).
